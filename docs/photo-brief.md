# MS Accountants — Team Photography Brief

For Dr Sridaran and the team, or any photographer engaged for the shoot.
The new website is designed around real portraits. Until these arrive, the
site shows an elegant typographic stand-in (the person's initials), so there
is no rush, but the section reaches its full effect only with real faces.

## What we need

Seven photographs in total:

1. One portrait of each of the six team members (Dr Sridaran, Niroshi,
   Lakshika Subramaniam, Eshani, Lakshika Senaviratne, Anne).
2. Optionally, one relaxed group photograph of the whole team.

## How to shoot them (a phone in good light is genuinely enough)

- **Orientation and framing:** vertical (portrait) orientation. Head and
  shoulders, with a little space above the head and roughly chest-up in
  frame. Keep the face centred; the website crops each image both as a
  tall rectangle and as a square, so avoid placing the face near an edge.
- **Background:** the same background for all six. A plain, warm, neutral
  wall (beige, cream, warm grey or a dark brown tone) is ideal. No windows
  behind the subject, no busy shelves, no green-screen look.
- **Light:** soft daylight from a window to one side of the face, or a
  single soft lamp. Everyone should be lit the same way. Avoid overhead
  office fluorescents and on-camera flash.
- **Expression and dress:** natural and at ease; a slight smile reads
  better than a formal stare. Business attire, as worn for client meetings.
- **Quality:** the largest size the camera produces. At minimum 2400 pixels
  on the shorter edge. No filters, no beauty mode, no AI retouching.
