## 2. VOICE & WRITING RULES (apply to all copy)

- **Professional but human.** Confident, plain-spoken, warm.
- **No jargon, no buzzwords** (avoid "streamline," "optimise," "innovative," "solutions").
- **No exclamation points.**
- **Benefits over features** — every credential gets a "which means for you" pivot.
- **Specific over vague** — numbers and named firms beat adjectives.
- **Active voice. Confident, not qualified** (cut "almost," "very," "really").
- **Honest** — no invented stats, awards, or testimonials.
- **Authority earned through specifics, then spent through approachability** — pair every authority signal with a warmth signal so the site never reads as cold/eminent.
- **Numbers consistency:** use **"30 years" for Dr Sridaran personally** everywhere; do not mix with the team's "nearly four decades" claim unless still accurate.
